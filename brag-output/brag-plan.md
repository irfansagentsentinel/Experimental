# Brag plan: Reef Sentinel

Made with `/brag-slim` from [latent-spaces/brag](https://github.com/latent-spaces/brag) (v0.6.0).

- **Input:** the company brief and the hotel pamphlet (no code project and no reachable website in this session).
- **Format:** vertical, 1080×1920, 30 fps.
- **Duration:** 22.67 s (34 beats at 90 BPM, 680 frames).
- **Tone:** `app-store`, kept restrained to match the pamphlet: clean feature cards, smooth slides.

## Angle

Bookings reach an island hotel from everywhere. Reef Sentinel puts them in one place, and the people who set it up are just around the corner.

- **Hook:** "Bookings from everywhere?" Booking sites, travel agents, walk-ins and phone calls land crooked, then line up: "All in one place."
- **Highlights:** stop double bookings; a faster front desk; support just around the corner.
- **Punchline:** price locked for 3 years when you join by 31 March 2027. See it now, switch when you're ready.

## Visual identity (from the pamphlet)

- Colours: lavender `#d7d0f4`, black `#101110`, purple `#ab87ed`, indigo `#3d3776`, card tint `#f6f4fb`.
- Type: Outfit, bold headlines with tight tracking, spaced capitals for labels.
- Motifs: the logo, drifting wave lines, black pills with a purple dot, rounded cards with lavender icon tiles.
- Scene changes rise as a wave, picking up the logo's wave.

## Notes

- **Show the thing.** There was no product source or live site to reuse, so the room chart, front desk and chat are rebuilt by hand in the pamphlet's design system. They show features the pamphlet marks "Live today".
- **Placeholder records.** Room numbers and guest counts are illustrative. No guest, hotel or agent names appear, and booking sites are labelled "Site 1" to "Site 3".
- **Claims.** Every claim and number is taken from the pamphlet. Nothing is invented.
- **Platform UI.** Everything meant to be read sits between y 250 and y 1536 and left of x 940.
- **Poster.** The reveal (scene 2), settled, is baked in as frame 0.
