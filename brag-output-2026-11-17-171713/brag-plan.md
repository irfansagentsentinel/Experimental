# Brag plan: Reef Sentinel (from the live site)

Made with `/brag-slim` from [latent-spaces/brag](https://github.com/latent-spaces/brag) (v0.6.0).

- **Input:** www.reefsentinel.com, loaded in headless Chromium and scrolled section by section.
- **Format:** vertical, 1080×1920, 30 fps.
- **Duration:** 22.67 s (34 beats at 90 BPM, 680 frames).
- **Tone:** `app-store`: clean feature cards, smooth slides. Restrained, like the site.
- **Look:** the pamphlet's lavender and black with Outfit type, as originally asked. The site itself uses Geist with indigo and gold; its real screenshots carry its UI colours into the video.

## Angle

Three ways to sell, one room inventory. Lead with the site's diagram, then show the real product doing the job: see every room, change a rate once and every channel follows, check a guest in and turn the folio into a GST invoice. End on the site's demo call.

## Notes

- **Placeholder records.** The site's screenshots show guest names and a hotel name. They may be demo data, but every one is replaced: guests become "Guest 1" to "Guest 5", and the hotel becomes "Your hotel". The folio is cropped to its dialog so the booking list behind it, which also shows names, is left out.
- **Claims.** Every line comes from the site, except the phone number (from the pamphlet) and two small illustrative toasts ("Checked in · Room S-201", "GST invoice ready"). The pamphlet's "30+ properties" and price lock are left out, since the site doesn't make those claims.
- **OTA names** are plain text from the site's channel list, with no logos.
- **Platform UI.** Everything meant to be read sits between y 250 and y 1536 and left of x 940 in the lower half.
- **Poster.** The reveal (scene 2), settled, is baked in as frame 0.
